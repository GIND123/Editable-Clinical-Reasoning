# Instructions for study participants (draft)

You will review about 120 short clinical cases from published case reports. For each case, an AI system proposes a diagnosis. Sometimes it shows its reasoning, as a diagram or as text; sometimes it shows only the answer.

**The AI is sometimes wrong.** Your task is to reach the best final diagnosis, using the AI as you would a colleague's suggestion.

For each case:
1. Read the case.
2. Look at the AI's suggestion.
3. Either **accept** it or **correct** it.
   - When you see a **reasoning diagram**, you can click any finding or reasoning step to change it: remove it, mark it absent, add a finding, or change its weight. The diagnosis updates immediately. If you state something that contradicts the case text, the system will point to the conflicting text. You can still override it.
   - Otherwise, type your corrected diagnosis.
4. Enter your final diagnosis and your confidence (0–100).

There is no time limit, but please work at your normal clinical pace. Do not use external resources. Your responses are pseudonymous and are used only for research.
